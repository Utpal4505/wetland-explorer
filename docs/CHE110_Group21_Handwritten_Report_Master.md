## 1. Introduction (Topic & Problem Statement)

### 1.1 Definition & Scope of Wetlands
* **Ramsar Convention Definition (Article 1.1):** Wetlands are *"areas of marsh, fen, peatland or water, whether natural or artificial, permanent or temporary, with water that is static or flowing, fresh, brackish or salt, including areas of marine water the depth of which at low tide does not exceed six metres."*
* **Spectrum in India:** Inland marshes, floodplains, coastal lagoons, mangroves, and high-altitude alpine lakes.
* **National Inventory:** India has **98 designated Ramsar Sites** (as of 30 January 2026, Ramsar Secretariat), covering over 1.35 million hectares.

### 1.2 Environmental Chemistry Role (CHE110 Core)
* **Biological Kidneys (Denitrification):** Under anoxic conditions in waterlogged sediments, bacteria reduce agricultural nitrate runoff into nitrogen gas:
  $$\text{NO}_3^- \xrightarrow{\text{reduction}} \text{NO}_2^- \rightarrow \text{NO} \rightarrow \text{N}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{N}_2\uparrow$$
  *(Note: Incomplete reactions can release $\text{N}_2\text{O}$, a potent greenhouse gas).*
* **Heavy Metal Attenuation:** Macrophytes (*Phragmites*, *Typha*) take up and retain heavy metals ($\text{Pb, Cd, As}$) in roots, up to natural saturation limits.
* **Carbon Accumulation:** Waterlogged soils retard organic decomposition, storing organic carbon over centuries.
* **Hydrological Buffering:** Absorbs monsoon flood peaks and recharges depleted regional groundwater aquifers.
* **⚠️ Ecological Limits:** Severe industrial effluents and altered hydrology overwhelm wetland self-purification capacities.

### 1.3 Problem Statement: Why India's Wetlands Are Disappearing
1. **The "Wasteland" Fallacy:** Historically viewed as unproductive swamps, encouraging solid waste dumping and encroachment.
2. **Eutrophication:** Fertilizer runoff ($NPK$) triggers severe algal blooms, depleting Dissolved Oxygen ($\text{DO} < 3\text{ mg/L}$).
3. **Siltation & Invasive Weeds:** Catchment erosion fills beds with silt; Water Hyacinth (*Eichhornia crassipes*) blocks sunlight.
4. **Awareness Gap:** Scientific data is buried in dense gazettes; students and citizens lack accessible tools to monitor local wetlands.

## 2. Review of Literature

1. **Ramsar Convention Secretariat (1971, updated 2026) — *Strategic Framework & Montreux Record*:**  
   * Establishes 9 international criteria for wetland conservation.
   * Maintains the **Montreux Record** for wetlands undergoing adverse ecological change.
   * In India, **Keoladeo National Park** and **Loktak Lake** remain on the record; **Chilika Lake** was restored and delisted in 2002.

2. **Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (MoEFCC) (2017) — *Wetlands Rules, 2017*:**  
   * Applies to Ramsar Sites and wetlands notified by State Wetland Authorities (SWAs).
   * Prohibits: setting up non-ecological industries in buffer zones, dumping untreated waste/sewage, and unauthorized reclamation.
   * Backed by the **Amrit Dharohar Initiative (2023)** to promote community conservation across Ramsar sites.

3. **Bassi, N., Kumar, M. D., Sharma, A., & Pardha-Saradhi, P. (2014) (Journal of Hydrology: Regional Studies):**  
   * Landmark study documenting rapid spatial shrinkage and pollution in urban wetlands (Deepor Beel, Bhoj Wetland).
   * Concludes that top-down legislation must be paired with public participatory monitoring and transparent digital information.

4. **WWF-India & Wildlife Institute of India (2022) — *Harike Lake Conservation Studies*:**  
   * Documents acute pressures at Harike Wetland (Ramsar Site #462, Punjab): heavy water hyacinth mats, siltation, and toxic industrial effluents carried via the Sutlej River.
   * Highlights the connected Beas river basin as the only Indian refuge for the endangered **Indus River Dolphin** (*Platanista gangetica minor*).

5. **Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) (2020) — *National Water Quality Assessment*:**  
   * Recorded high chemical nitrates, phosphates, and heavy metals in major lakes, underscoring the failure of municipal wastewater treatment.

   * Translates complex coordinates and impenetrable government gazettes into an intuitive, visual map accessible to any student on a phone or laptop.
